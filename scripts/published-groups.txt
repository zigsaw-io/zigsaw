# The published recipes (scripts/published-recipes.txt), in the groups that
# the Reproduce workflow checks in parallel jobs, one job a group:
# GROUP=<group> tests/published.sh. Each recipe is in exactly one group, which
# tests/published.sh checks. A group builds its recipes in the order
# published-recipes.txt gives, and pulls the images they pin that other
# groups build from the registry, so a group needn't hold what its recipes
# build with; grouping an image with the recipes that pin it only saves the
# pull. Groups are balanced by how long their builds took on a runner
# (minutes, after iteration 32).
#
#   <group>: <recipe>...      (a group may take several lines)
#
# `skip` is left out of CI: GTK's images, the Perl, cargo-c and gettext
# images only they build with, and the apps on them, until they're stable
# locally. GTK's SDK alone is a quarter of an hour of building.
